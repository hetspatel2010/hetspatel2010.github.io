About Het

The complete third-person biography supplied for profile and search use.

Het Patel is a 16-year-old high school student, Indian app developer, and technology enthusiast based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Deeply passionate about software engineering and emerging technologies, he is the creator and developer of Zenith Daily List, a productivity application published on the Google Play Store.

Proficient in Python and Flutter, Het builds cross-platform mobile applications and explores the frontiers of Generative AI and blockchain technology. He is actively learning about decentralized systems, studying how blockchain networks operate, and working toward designing and building his own blockchain architectures.

Beyond software development, Het is heavily involved in low-level system customization and hardware experimentation. His hands-on technical work includes Android rooting, bootloader unlocking, system debugging, flashing custom Google Pixel ROMs, and configuring macOS on PC hardware. As a verified Xiaomi HyperOS Beta Tester (Mi Pilot) and an active Level 3 member of the Xiaomi Community (hetpatel), he tests early firmware releases and contributes feedback within the Xiaomi ecosystem.

An active member of the developer community, Het participates in Google Developer Group (GDG Cloud Ahmedabad & GDG Gandhinagar) and the Google Developer Program (Google I/O 2026 Registered, Google Cloud Arcade 2026). He also holds technical certifications including Building with the Claude API (Anthropic), NASA Open Science 101, and cloud and data analytics simulations.
